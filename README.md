## Overview

Somewhere is a long-form musical storytelling project structured in books and seasons.

- Book 1 (Seasons 1–4) — Complete  
- Book 2 — In progress  

Each song is a chapter in a coherent narrative exploring memory, identity and reality.

The Creative Process Behind Somewhere

Somewhere is an AI-assisted narrative musical saga conceived and artistically directed by Sébastien Loiret, initiated on January 29, 2025.

The project emerged through sustained creative collaboration between Sébastien Loiret and ChatGPT, with music generated using Suno and occasional consultation with Gemini.

Its narrative universe, characters, songs, and long-term structure have developed through an iterative process of human decisions, AI-generated proposals, collaborative writing, critical evaluation, and artistic refinement.

Sébastien Loiret acknowledges that Somewhere, in its present form and scale, would not have been possible without these artificial intelligence systems.

The project documents not only a fictional universe told through music, but also an evolving method of human–AI creative collaboration.
